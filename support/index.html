SUPPORT

How can we help?

OneLoop is a personal medication reminder. There is no in-app chat — email is the fastest way to reach us. An optional account is available in the app for cloud backup.

Email support

Use a real inbox you check. Include your iOS version and a short description of what happened.

support@oneloop.app

Typical reply: within 2 business days.

Write an email

Reminders are not arriving

OneLoop uses local notifications. Open Settings in the app, turn on Medication reminders, and allow notifications if iOS asks. If permission was denied, use Open Settings and enable notifications for OneLoop. Delivery also depends on Low Power Mode, Focus, and whether the device is powered on.

Where is my data stored?

On your device by default. Optional cloud backup is available when you sign in. Widget data is shared only with the OneLoop widget through an App Group on the same iPhone. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my information?

Delete individual medications in the app, or delete the app to remove local OneLoop data. Device backups you keep (such as iCloud Backup) may still contain app data until those backups expire or are removed.

Is OneLoop a medical device?

No. It is a personal organizer for schedules you enter yourself. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace your clinician or pharmacist. Read the medical disclaimer.

The widget looks empty

Add at least one medication with a schedule, then add the Medication Progress widget. It refreshes from the same on-device data as the app.
