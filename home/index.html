TUTORIAL

Add a medication in three steps.

OneLoop is built around a simple loop: add what you take, get reminded, mark it done, and keep a history you can trust.

1

Tap +

Open the center + on the bottom menu, or the + in the Today toolbar, to start a new medication.

2

Enter dose details

Name, form (pill, injection, cream), amount, first reminder time, and how many times per day — including staged schedule changes.

3

Follow Today

Mark doses taken, snooze when needed, browse Schedule by day, and review History — even after you remove a medication from your list.

Enable notifications in Settings if you want local reminders on your iPhone. You can use OneLoop fully offline, or create an account later for optional cloud backup.

WIDGETS ON iOS

Home Screen and Lock Screen at a glance.

OneLoop includes WidgetKit surfaces so you can check your next medication and today’s progress without opening the app.

▣

Home Screen

Small and medium widgets show the next dose, dosage, reminder time, and how many doses you’ve logged today.

◻

Lock Screen

Circular, rectangular, and inline widgets keep the next reminder visible when your phone is locked.

＋

How to add

Touch and hold the Home or Lock Screen → Edit → Add Widget → search OneLoop → place the size you want.
